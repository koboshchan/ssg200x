The casino setting is houseEdgeBps, an integer between -1000 and 1000 inclusive. One basis point equals 0.01 percent. Missing or invalid persisted settings normalize to zero; admin updates reject invalid values. The existing casino settings endpoint requires administrator authorization, revision compare-and-swap, and an audit record. Omitting the optional field retains its current setting. This range limits the gross-return scale to 90–110 percent, but does not imply that an individual win cannot exceed a player's stake or the house's current balance.

Dice, Mines, Hi-Lo, Chicken Road and all three Plinko risks previously had nominal 98 percent RTP, reduced further by floor rounding. New wagers normalize to a fair probability-weighted gross return first, then apply the configured scale once. At -10 percent edge their exact expected RTP is 110 percent; at zero it is 100 percent; at +2 percent it is 98 percent. Sequential games apply the adjustment once, not on each step. Plinko retains eight fair binary bounces and independent per-risk normalization.

Roulette retains all 37 equally likely pockets, including zero. Previously every conventional bet returned 36/37 of its stake in expectation, approximately 97.297297 percent, while noncanonical chip totals could silently forfeit. New straight bets have a fair gross multiplier of 37; dozen/column bets 37/12; outside bets 37/18. Each is multiplied by (10000-houseEdgeBps)/10000. Their exact expected RTP at -10, zero and +2 percent is therefore 110, 100 and 98 percent respectively. All valid whole-dollar placements are payable under the same odds; historical dead placements remain historical, not a newly reachable betting policy.

New payout settlement uses unbiased stochastic cent rounding, not floor rounding. For exact rational x cents, it pays floor(x) with probability 1-frac(x) and ceil(x) with probability frac(x). Rejection-sampled integer randomness avoids floating-point and modulo bias. The resulting expectation equals x exactly. A wager settles once inside the existing room/wallet CAS, and history reuses its actual payout. Previews must not draw settlement randomness. Neither deterministic rounding down nor always rounding up can claim exact RTP.

Accepted wagers persist their setting. Admin changes affect subsequent wagers, not active ones. Rebet is a new wager. Explicit roulette bet changes and blackjack betting-period replacements accept the then-current setting. Blackjack split and double retain their original hand setting. Existing minigames without snapshots preserve their 98 percent rational odds and legacy floor rounding; existing roulette wagers preserve their conventional multiplier and dead-marker behavior.

Blackjack is not claimed to have exact 100 percent RTP at zero. Its six-deck S17 rules, Black 78 priority, five-card Charlie, splits, doubles, player strategy and timeout forfeiture determine its baseline EV. Its configured signed edge is an explicit gross-return adjustment relative to that baseline, including pushes and naturals. Thus a +2 percent setting means 0.98 times the baseline expected gross return, not necessarily 98 percent of total stakes. A -10 percent setting means 1.10 times baseline. New bets no longer select hidden weighted private cards based on chip composition. Already active historical private hands keep their persisted stream for migration compatibility. An exact optimal-strategy analysis for this nonstandard rule set has not been established.

Poker and Dou Dizhu are player-versus-player with no game rake to remove. Prediction fills use external market bid/ask prices with no added house trading fee; their market risk and external spread cannot be made fair coin flips through a payout adjustment. Trading/perpetual fees and cosmetic poker throw purchases are not house-banked game payouts and are unchanged. Coinflip, Crash, Limbo, Wheel, Slots and Keno occur in the feature catalog but have no implemented wager engine/routes in this revision, so no invented RTP is assigned.

Exact new engine tests enumerate roulette pockets, Plinko binomial weights, dice targets, sequential rational odds and stochastic cent endpoint classes at signed settings. These are mathematical and engine checks, not substitutes for authenticated isolated application QA. Production deployment requires separate approval.
